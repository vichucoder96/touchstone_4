About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery began with a home oven, a jar of sourdough starter, and a simple idea: good bread brings people together. What started as weekend loaves shared with friends and neighbors grew into a small shop on Main Street, where the lights come on before sunrise and the first bake is always for the people who live right around the corner.

Today we are still a small, family-run bakery. We know our customers by name, we remember who likes their loaf sliced, and we are proud to be part of the neighborhood.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

Great baking starts with great ingredients. Wherever we can, we buy from nearby farms and mills, and we choose butter, eggs, and flour from producers we trust.

  • Local first: seasonal fruit, dairy, and eggs from regional farms.
  • Simple ingredients: flour, water, salt, and time for our breads.
  • Made by hand: small batches, shaped and scored by our bakers.
  • Less waste: day-old bread goes to community food programs.
