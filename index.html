What We’re Known For

Neighbors know us for crusty, slow-fermented breads, buttery pastries, and celebration cakes made to order. Our most loved loaf is the one we are proudest of.

  • Hand-shaped breads baked fresh every morning
  • Pastries and cookies made from scratch
  • Custom cakes for birthdays, weddings, and community events
  • Pre-orders for families, offices, and event planners
