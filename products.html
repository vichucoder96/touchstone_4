    Breads

    Our loaves are slow-fermented for deep flavor and a crackling crust. We bake a rotating selection every day, so ask what just came out of the oven.

    • Signature Country Loaf: hand-shaped with a golden crust and chewy crumb.
    • Seeded Multigrain: topped with pumpkin, flax, and sunflower seeds.
    • Rustic White Boule: simple, soft, and perfect for sandwiches.

    Price range: $6 – $10 per loaf

    Pastries & Cookies

    From flaky morning pastries to cookies baked for the after-school crowd, our sweet case is stocked fresh every day.

    • Brown Butter Chocolate Chunk Cookies: crisp edges and gooey centers.
    • Glazed Honey Buns: soft, lightly sweet, and iced by hand.
    • Sugar-Dusted Sandwich Cookies: filled with cream and finished with pearl sugar.

    Price range: $3 – $6 each, or $18 – $32 per dozen

    Cakes

    Celebrating something special? We bake cakes for birthdays, weddings, and neighborhood gatherings. Cakes are made to order, so please request yours at least five days in advance.

    • Classic Layer Cakes: vanilla, chocolate, or lemon with buttercream.
    • Seasonal Fruit Cakes: made with fruit from local growers.
    • Cupcakes by the Dozen: great for parties and office events.

    Price range: $35 – $120 depending on size and decoration

    Ready to Order?

    Planning an event or want to be sure your favorites are waiting for you? Send a pre-order request and tell us about any allergies.
